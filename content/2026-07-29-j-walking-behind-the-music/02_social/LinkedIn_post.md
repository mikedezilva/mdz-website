**As a creator and producer, sometimes your best move is to just get out of the way.**

I recently brought Shaun Perera and Shane Vincent into the studio together for the first time to work on my upcoming track, "J Walking". 

I've worked with both of them individually on stage and in the booth. But when I played them the hook together? Complete opposite reactions.

Shaun instantly went into a deep, spiritual, peace-driven headspace. 
Shane immediately felt the aggression and yelled, "Rebelliooooonn!" 

The chemistry was hilarious. They were bouncing off each other’s completely different wavelengths, and we even ended up going down a rabbit hole discussing the Illuminati (yes, I have it on video).

The biggest lesson for anyone leading a creative project:
**You don't always need to control the session. Sometimes you just need to set the environment, hit record, and let the talent express their truest energy.**

"J Walking" drops soon on all major platforms. Stay tuned. 🎵
